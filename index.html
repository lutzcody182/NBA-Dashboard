Daily leaders

The best lines from one night of games, with the efficiency and impact numbers behind them, and the changes in role that tend to carry into the next games. Rate leaders need a minimum workload so a 2-for-2 night doesn't top the list.

Worth knowing going forward

Minutes and starting roles change slowly and carry into future games far better than one night's shooting does, so these are the changes to watch. "Previous 10" is each player's last 10 games before this one, this season and last.
